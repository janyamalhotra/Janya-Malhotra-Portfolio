About Me

I'm someone who tends to notice things, how people navigate spaces, the small frictions in everyday routines, and the unspoken ways objects and systems shape our behavior. That natural curiosity is what drew me to design in the first place.

As a designer, I'm interested in the space between understanding a problem deeply and translating that understanding into tangible, thoughtful artifacts. Whether working on interaction models, physical-digital systems, or speculative services, I approach design with care for nuance, rhythm, and clarity.
