Indian Sign Language Practice Platform

Overview

A web-based practice platform for beginner Indian Sign Language learners. The platform is not intended to teach ISL or replace a tutor. Developed as an individual four-week project, it combines webcam-based gesture recognition, interactive practice and real-time feedback to make independent sign language practice more engaging and accessible.

Project Type Product Design
My Role Individual Project
Project Timeline 2026 (4 weeks)
Tools Claude, Antigravity, ChatGPT

Problem

Learning ISL requires consistent practice and repetition, but learners may only interact with a tutor once a week or less. Between sessions, there is often no easy way to practice signing, check whether a gesture is being performed correctly, or identify which vocabulary is being forgotten.

Existing learning resources can help users discover signs, but there is an opportunity for a more interactive practice tool that allows learners to actively sign, receive feedback, and repeatedly practice their weaker vocabulary.

  • Limited opportunities to practice between tutor sessions.
  • Difficulty retaining previously learnt signs without regular repetition.
  • Lack of immediate feedback when practicing independently.
  • Difficulty identifying which signs need more practice.

The Design

The platform has been designed around flexibility, simplicity, and real-time interaction. Learners can customize their practice based on their vocabulary, pace, and learning needs, while live gesture recognition provides immediate feedback and correction. By bringing practice, progress tracking, personalized review, and dataset management into one mobile-friendly experience, it creates a seamless way to practice ISL.

The interface has been designed to make ISL practice feel simple, approachable and low-pressure, while keeping the signing experience at the center.

Home Page

The platform is organized into interconnected tools that support different stages of the learning process. Users can create gesture samples, manage vocabulary and practice signs through guided exercises or more open-ended interactions.

Page 1. STUDIO

Studio allows users to create gesture samples using hand landmarks captured through the webcam. These samples can then be explored and organised through the Dataset Viewer, where vocabulary can be managed, curated, imported and exported.

Page 2. PRACTICE

The Practice module Pulls from the active-word pool and quizzes the user. It shows a target word, matches your live gesture against the stored samples for that word, and tracks score, streak, and accuracy. Users can engage in practice sessions without the need of anyone else.

Page 3. STREAM

Stream allows users to practise signs through more open-ended webcam recognition rather than following a fixed quiz. Hold to Confirm gives users control over recognized gestures, while Sentence Builder extends the experience by allowing signs to be combined into sentences with speech output.

Page 4. DATASET VIEWER

A dedicated space to explore the ISL vocabulary behind the platform. Users can browse individual signs, view their recorded samples, and understand how the dataset grows over time.

Ending Thoughts

Together, the four pages form one loop: Studio feeds data in, Dataset lets you curate what's usable, Practice and Stream are the two ways to consume that same data: one structured for learning, one open-ended for real use.
